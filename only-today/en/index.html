Only Today

A sober day counter with an AI mentor. Just for today — not forever, not a year.

What it is

An app for anyone who decided to stop. Alcohol, drugs, smoking, vaping, gambling, pornography, overeating — you pick yours on first launch and the app adapts to it.

The tracker counts every day and hour. The mentor is there any time. The exercises follow a rehab centre programme. Interface in nine languages.

What you get

Day counter

See how long you have held on — days, hours, minutes. Alongside it, what is happening in your body and brain right now: when the fog lifts, when sleep comes back, when your system starts to repair itself.

AI mentor

Write whatever is bothering you, at any hour. No lectures, no scare tactics. It helps you break down a craving, plan the day, get through the evening. It answers by voice if reading is hard.

Feelings journal

Write down what is going on inside. Over time you see what repeats and what sets you off.

Craving journal

Log when the urge hit and what came before it. That is how personal triggers surface.

Fifteen lectures

The nature of addiction, craving, withdrawal, codependency, character defects, emotional intelligence, maturity, goals, recovery.

Daily plan

Five concrete things for today. In the evening you mark what worked.

Recovery map

See the whole road and where you stand on it.

SOS and passcode

A button for the moment it hits you right now. And a code so nobody opens the app without you.

Straight talk

Your data stays on your phone. No sign-up, no account, no email.

Only Today does not replace a doctor, an addiction specialist or a therapist. It is support and self-help. Severe withdrawal needs medical care, not an app.

Frequently asked

Which addictions does it work for?

Alcohol, drugs, smoking, vaping, gambling, pornography, overeating and others. You pick yours on first launch.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no email. Data stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

What does the AI mentor do?

It answers at any hour, helps you break down a craving, plan the day and get through the evening. It replies in text and by voice.

How much does it cost?

The counter, journals, lectures and exercises are free. Five mentor messages a day are free. A subscription removes the message limit.

Does it replace treatment?

No. It is support and self-help. Severe withdrawal needs medical care.
